## 1. Executive Recommendation

Build **ACP SEO Edition** as a **$325/month core SaaS tier** ($3.25K annually) with a clear upsell path to agency/enterprise tiers at $1K+/month. This positions the product between budget tools (Frase $45, Writesonic $16) and premium optimizers (Clearscope $189, Rankability $166–199), while staying well below agency-managed AI SEO retainers ($2K–$25K/mo).

**Why $325/mo is the right anchor:**
- It signals "serious tool" without requiring a CFO sign-off.
- It is ~3× Surfer's entry price and ~2× Frase, justified by agentic automation (the ACP differentiator).
- It leaves headroom for a $99–149 starter tier and a $799+ agency tier.
- It matches Jon's "$3.25 but architected to be a 10+ when it grows up" framing.

## 2. Market Evidence Summary

### 2.1 AI SEO *Services* (agency retainers)
From Stackmatix (2026):

| Tier | Monthly Cost | Best For | Typical Deliverables |
|------|-------------:|----------|---------------------|
| Starter | $2,000–$5,000 | Small business / local SEO | AI Overview optimization for 10–20 keywords, basic schema, 2–4 content pieces/mo, GEO audit |
| Growth | $5,000–$12,000 | Mid-market | Full AEO/GEO strategy, 8–12 content pieces/mo, technical SEO, entity optimization, AI citation tracking |
| Enterprise | $12,000–$25,000+ | Large orgs / multi-location | Comprehensive AIEO strategy, 20+ content pieces, custom AI answer-engine monitoring, dedicated strategist |

**Insight:** The $2K/mo floor for managed services creates a large gap for a self-serve/automated platform that delivers 60–70% of the output at ~15% of the cost.

### 2.2 AI SEO *Software* (self-serve tools)
From Sight AI and Rankability roundups:

| Tool | Starting Price | Model | Key Differentiator |
|------|---------------:|-------|--------------------|
| Frase | $45/mo | Unlimited on Team tier ($115) | AI briefs + writing, budget all-in-one |
| Surfer SEO | $89–99/mo | Per article credits | Live content score, NLP terms, strong Docs/WP integration |
| Clearscope | $189/mo | Per report (100 reports) | A–F content grade, intent-focused, unlimited seats |
| Rankability | $166–199/mo | All-in-one | IBM Watson + Google NLU keyword recommendations, weekly coaching |
| MarketMuse | $99–149/mo | Inventory-based | Topic authority, gap analysis, strategic clusters |
| Writesonic | $16/mo | Credits | Cheap AI article writer + SERP analysis |
| AirOps | $199/mo | Tasks/workflows | Programmatic SEO, AI search visibility workflows |
| Semrush SWA | $249/mo (bundled) | Bundled with Guru | Real-time scoring inside Semrush ecosystem |
| Cogny | ~$9/mo (per Jon) | ? | Ultra-budget entry point |

**Insight:** The market clusters into three price bands:
1. **Budget ($9–49/mo):** Frase, Writesonic, Cogny — high volume, lower precision.
2. **Pro ($89–249/mo):** Surfer, Clearscope, Rankability, MarketMuse, AirOps — optimization-first.
3. **Managed ($2K–25K/mo):** Agencies — strategy + execution + reporting.

ACP SEO Edition should own the **Pro band with an automation layer** (agents doing research, briefs, drafts, optimization, and publishing orchestration).

## 4. Phase 1 Recommendation: "Growth Lite"

Ryan's guidance: *start simple, foundation solid, scalable.* Jon's: *$3.25 but architected to be a 10+ when it grows up.*

### Phase 1 Scope (MVP — ship the $325 tier skeleton)

**Must-have for v1:**
1. **Keyword research module** — input a seed topic, get keyword list with difficulty, intent, and volume estimates.
2. **AI content briefs** — SERP-derived outline with headings, questions, and NLP terms.
3. **Content editor with real-time score** — the "score-as-you-write" experience (Surfer's core UX).
4. **Single-agent SEO assistant** — one ACP agent that can run research, generate briefs, and draft content.
5. **1 project + 3 seats** — enough for a small team to validate.
6. **WordPress / Google Docs export** — publish path.

**Explicitly out of scope for v1:**
- Multi-project workspaces
- White-label reporting
- AI answer-engine visibility tracking (ChatGPT/Perplexity monitoring)
- Autopilot scheduled runs
- Custom AI models / IBM Watson integration

**Why this scope:**
- It validates the core loop (keyword → brief → draft → score → publish) for $149–325/mo buyers.
- It avoids the $2K/mo agency-retainer complexity trap.
- It leaves the "10+ when it grows up" features as a clear expansion roadmap.

## 5. Keyword-Recommendation Strategy

Jon flagged keyword recommendation as the "heart of the game" and the hardest piece of equipment to replicate. Two options:

### Option A: Build in-house (recommended for Phase 1)
- Combine Google NLP API + SERP scraping + internal clickstream data.
- Target "80% as good as Watson at 20% of the cost."
- Pros:可控 cost, no vendor lock-in, defensible IP.
- Cons: Takes longer, needs data feedback loop.

### Option B: Integrate IBM Watson / Google NLU
- Rankability's dual-NLP approach is their moat.
- Pros: Faster time-to-market, marketing credibility.
- Cons: Vendor cost, API dependency, may not materially outperform a well-tuned in-house model for our target segment.

**BAPert recommendation:** Start with Option A. Use Watson/Google NLU only as a *benchmark* during v1, then decide if the uplift justifies the cost before the Agency tier. This aligns with Ryan's "foundation solid" directive.

## 6. Risks & Assumptions

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Pricing too high vs. Frase/Surfer | Anchor on agentic automation + multi-step workflow, not just optimization. |
| Pricing too low to support AI costs | Set usage caps per tier; monitor COGS per brief/article. |
| Keyword data accuracy | Validate against third-party tools (Ahrefs/Semrush samples) before launch. |
| AI content quality | Human-in-the-loop editor is mandatory; score is a guide, not a guarantee. |
| Rankability/Surfer respond with bundled pricing | Win on workflow automation, not feature parity. |
