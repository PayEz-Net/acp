# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 2026

**Contact:** privacy@payez.com

---

## Overview

ACP (Agent Collaboration Platform) is a desktop application that helps developers coordinate AI agents. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## What We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address (used for magic-link authentication)
- Display name
- OAuth profile data (if you sign in with Google)

### Project Metadata
- Project names and settings
- Team composition (which agents are assigned to which projects)
- Agent configuration (names, prompts, roles — **not** your code)

### Agent Mail
- Subject lines and routing metadata
- Mail bodies are processed for delivery but are not used for model training

### Usage Telemetry
- App version and crash reports
- Feature usage (anonymized)
- Performance metrics

## What We Do NOT Collect

- **Your source code** — ACP runs agents locally on your machine. We do not upload your repositories.
- **Terminal output** — Agent execution happens in local PTY shells.
- **File contents** — Unless explicitly shared via agent mail, your files stay on your device.

## How We Use Your Data

- **Authentication** — to verify your identity and maintain your session
- **Service delivery** — to route agent mail and sync project state across your devices
- **Improvement** — anonymized telemetry helps us prioritize features and fix bugs
- **Support** — to respond to help requests

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only:
- With service providers (hosting, analytics) under strict confidentiality
- When legally required (subpoena, court order)
- In anonymized/aggregated form for product improvement

## Data Retention

- Account data: retained while your account is active
- Project metadata: retained until you delete the project
- Agent mail: retained for 90 days, then purged
- Crash logs: retained for 30 days

## Your Rights

You can:
- Export your project data
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Request a copy of your personal data
- Opt out of telemetry (Settings → Privacy)

To exercise these rights, email privacy@payez.com.
